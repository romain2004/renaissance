About

ROMAIN
MAULAVÉ
Filmmaker · France & New York

At 18, I left everything behind to travel the world with nothing but a camera. From the streets of Bangkok to the runways of New York Fashion Week, I spent years chasing light, documenting moments, and developing an eye for the extraordinary in the everyday.

After living in New York and shooting for international fashion platforms, I brought that cinematic vision back to the South of France, where the light is unlike anywhere else on earth.

Today I work with private clients in the Gulf of Saint-Tropez, creating films and photography that don't just document your experience. They make it legendary.
