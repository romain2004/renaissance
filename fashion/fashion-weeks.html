What is
GFC?

Global Fashion Collective is an international platform dedicated to giving emerging designers access to runway shows during the world's biggest fashion weeks — New York, Milan, Paris, Tokyo.

Each season, GFC offers independent designers a real runway, a real audience, and a real camera — not a side-event. Five shows, one night: the moment a garment stops being a project and becomes something the world sees for the first time.
